# Developer Resources
Being a junior developer can be challenging; it can be the first time you are working on a large project or using a language at an advanced level.
However, there is a massive amount of tribal knowledge that developers simply pick up with experience or by working on a specific team.
I am putting this guide together to aggregate many beneficial guides, resources, and more that may not be included in your average CS curriculum.

**Note this will be continuously updated. Check-in every now and then to get the latest articles or resources!**

[Conventional Commits](https://www.conventionalcommits.org/en/v1.0.0/) are a way to write smart git commit messages. Many
projects use this convention, but even if they don't, you will at least have a template for structuring a commit message
and following SemVer.
